What is SweetCase?

A travel-culture quiz shaped like a digital passport: answer questions about how the world's cities really work, complete levels, and collect stamps. Cities on six continents, in three languages (English, Italiano, Español). The quiz itself plays offline; a free account keeps your progress backed up and lets you continue on another device.
