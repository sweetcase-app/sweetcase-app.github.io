What is SweetCase?

A travel-culture quiz shaped like a digital passport: answer questions about how the world's cities really work, complete levels, and collect Bronze, Silver and Gold stamps. Cities on six continents, six languages (English, Italiano, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch), fully offline, no account needed.
